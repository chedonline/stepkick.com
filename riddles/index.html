📺 Show it on the TV

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone.
  2. Swipe down from the top-right → Screen Mirroring → pick the Apple TV.
  3. Hold the phone upright or sideways. Upright works fine; sideways just fills more of the TV.
  4. Tap 🎤 at the top left to check the sound and the mic before you play.
  5. If the mic doesn't hear anything: Settings → General → Keyboard → turn on Enable Dictation, and allow the mic for this site.

🎤 Sound & mic test
